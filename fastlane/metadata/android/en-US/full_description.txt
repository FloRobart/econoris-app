Econoris answers a single question, the one that really matters: how much can I still spend this month?

• Left to spend: all your income of the month, minus all your expenses, including the ones that have not happened yet. The amount shown is really available.
• A financial month that follows your salary: your budget starts when your pay arrives, not on the 1st of the month.
• Upcoming operations: a planned purchase or bill is already deducted from what you have left to spend.
• Subscriptions: entered once with their recurrence (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly), with their total monthly cost.
• Money management index: a single figure to judge your month, without penalizing savings.
• More than 50 ready-to-use categories, light or dark theme.

Sign-in needs no password: a one-time code is sent by email. Your data is stored on the Econoris servers, so you find it again in the web application (app.econoris.fr) and in the browser extension.

Free, without ads, under the GPL-3.0 free license.

The interface is in French, and the amounts in euros.
